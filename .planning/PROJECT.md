# Project: Chhaigaon Udyami (छैगांव उद्यमी)

## Mission
Empowering rural and grassroots entrepreneurs in Chhaigaon Makhan and Khandwa (Madhya Pradesh) with high-quality localized vocational courses, verified government scheme access, business incubation toolkits, and mentor/market linkages.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Curated Video Courses & Quizzes**: DRM protected video streaming (VdoCipher) and modular assessments with verifiable digital certificates.
2. **Government Scheme Directory**: Curated portal of central and state MSME / agriculture subsidies and financial assistance programs.
3. **Startup Toolkits & Resources**: Step-by-step guides, DPR templates, and business modeling tools for local agro-processing, dairy, and rural enterprises.
4. **Market & Mentorship Linkage**: Connecting local entrepreneurs to mentors and B2B buyers.
5. **Secure Authentication & Payments**: Supabase SSR authentication and Razorpay gateway integration.

## Key Stakeholders
- **Grassroots Learners / Entrepreneurs**: Access courses, apply for schemes, download toolkits, track learning.
- **Trainers & Content Creators**: Publish course modules, lessons, and quizzes.
- **Administrators**: Verify enrollments, manage certificates, audit platform transactions and content.
