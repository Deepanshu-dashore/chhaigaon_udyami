# World-Class EdTech Design System (Coursera & IBM Skills Reference)

This ruleset establishes the design principles, card anatomy, and visual hierarchy inspired by **Coursera** and **IBM SkillsBuild**.

---

## 1. Palette & Tone

- **Hero & Primary Accent**: Coursera Royal Blue (`#0056d2`) with white text and clean pill buttons.
- **Canvas Surfaces**: Pure White (`#ffffff`), crisp Light Gray/Ice (`#f8fafc` / `#f1f5f9`).
- **Success / Subsidies**: Emerald Green (`#059669`).
- **Certificates / Badges**: Amber Gold (`#d97706`) & Sapphire Blue (`#0056d2`).
- **Borders & Dividers**: `1px solid #e2e8f0` (clean, minimal borders with zero heavy drop-shadows).

---

## 2. Key Components Architecture

### 1. Top Announcement Ticker (Coursera Style)
- Slim navy/blue top banner with bold microcopy and instant action link.

### 2. Search & Explore Bar
- Wide rounded search input with blue magnifying glass circular button and category quick-select dropdown.

### 3. Partner / Authority Logo Cloud
- Subtle "Learn from leading authorities & banks" heading above horizontal row of white pill badges with authority icons (NABARD, MSME, DIC, KVIC).

### 4. "Explore Roles" Enterprise Cards (Image 5)
- Category filter pills at top (`Popular`, `Dairy`, `Food Processing`, `Digital Marketing`).
- Card layout: Role title, 2-line description, estimated monthly earnings statistic (`₹45,000 - ₹1.2L मासिक आय`), authority badge, and clean illustration.

### 5. "Turn Learning into Proof" Certificate Fan (Image 2)
- Overlapping fan of 2-3 crisp certificate cards with blue security ribbon and QR verification badge.

### 6. Transparent Tier / Plan Cards (Image 3)
- 3 comparison cards with "Best Value" top badge, price display, feature checklists with green checkmarks, and solid/outline buttons.

### 7. Horizontal Milestone CTA Banner (Image 4)
- Rounded Royal Blue card with bold headline, white action button, and authentic photograph cutout.

### 8. Clean FAQ Accordions (Image 4 & 5)
- Border-separated accordion items with smooth chevron transitions.
